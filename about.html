教育願景

Our Vision

我們的願景

我們的願景，是希望在這個全球化的時代，透過最優質且創新的教學方式，培育立足台灣，放眼全球的世界公民。 我們致力於打造一個讓孩子成長、培養獨立人格的環境，從中培養他們的智慧、社交和情感技能。

Our vision is to empower the next generation of global citizens by providing a well-rounded education that combines the best and innovative teaching methods. We strive to create an environment where children can thrive and develop a strong sense of self, while also cultivating their intellectual, social, and emotional skills.

Features

教學特色

01
方案教學
Project-based Learning
我們的方案教學方法鼓勵學生關注現實世界的問題，運用他們的知識尋找實用的解決方案。這種實踐性的方法不僅加強了他們對各種學科的理解，還培養了團隊合作、溝通和解決問題的能力。
Our project-based learning approach encourages students to engage with real-world problems and apply their knowledge to find practical solutions. This hands-on methodology not only strengthens their understanding of various subjects but also promotes teamwork, communication, and problem-solving skills.
02
STREAM 素養教育
STREAM Education
我們將 STREAM（科學、科技、閱讀、工程、藝術和數學）教育素養融入課程，為學生提供符合課綱6大領域的知識和跨學科的學習素養。這種全面的教育模式可以有效激發孩子們的好奇心，培養他們對探索新知的渴望，並且符合資訊科技時代的需求。
STREAM (Science, Technology, Reading, Engineering, Arts, and Math) education is integrated into our curriculum, providing students with a balanced and interdisciplinary learning experience. This holistic approach encourages curiosity and fosters a lifelong passion for exploration and discovery.
03
沉浸式美語環境
Immersive English Environment
在艾倫戴爾幼兒園，我們提供沉浸式的美語學習環境，來幫助孩子們發展語言技能。我們經驗豐富的美語老師們提供了充滿愛和友善的學習環境，讓孩子們敢於用英語表達，也對自己的英語充滿信心。
At Arendelle Preschool, we offer an immersive English learning environment to help students develop their language skills. Our experienced teachers create a nurturing and supportive atmosphere, allowing children to build confidence in their English proficiency.
04
音樂與藝術
Music and Arts
音樂與藝術對孩子的美感、氣質養成非常重要。我們的專業藝術課程為學生提供各種機會來發揮他們的創造力，培養藝術才能。從太鼓課到各類樂器的學習以及音樂律動，到結合大自然、不同素材的美術創作活動，在專業的音樂和美術老師帶領下，能夠讓孩子們探索不同類型和風格的藝術，進而培養對藝術的鑑賞能力。
We believe that music and arts play an essential role in a child's overall development. Our comprehensive arts program offers various opportunities for students to express themselves creatively and develop their artistic talents. Our dedicated music and art instructors inspire children to explore different genres and styles, fostering a deep appreciation for the arts.
05
學習區探索
Learning Zones Exploration
艾倫戴爾幼兒園設計了獨特的學習區，為學生提供各種有趣且互動性強的活動。這些學習區激發孩子們的好奇心，使他們能夠自主探索不同主題，朝著有興趣的領域學習，並培養獨立思考的能力。
At Arendelle Preschool, we have designed unique learning zones that provide students with a variety of engaging and interactive activities. These zones stimulate curiosity and enable children to explore different topics in depth, promoting independent learning and critical thinking.
06
專業體適能活動
Professional Physical Fitness Activities
我們提供一系列專業的體適能活動，幫助孩子們增進肌力、身體協調能力和訓練平衡感。我們聘請的專業教練指導學生進行適合各年齡段的運動，確保在開心且安全的環境中進行活動。
We offer a range of professional physical fitness activities to help children develop their strength, coordination, and balance. Our qualified instructors guide students through age-appropriate exercises and activities, ensuring a fun and safe environment for physical development.
